# ALTAIS name and logo

The Apache License 2.0 covers the ALTAIS source code. It does not grant
permission to use the ALTAIS name or logo (see section 6 of the licence).
This policy describes how they may be used. The name is not a registered
trademark; this policy states the project's expectations.

## Allowed without asking

- Referring to the project by name, for example in articles, reviews,
  tutorials or talks.
- Saying that your product is compatible with ALTAIS or its data format, as
  long as the statement is true.
- Distributing unmodified official builds under the ALTAIS name.

## Not allowed

- Distributing a modified version under the name ALTAIS or a confusingly
  similar name. Forks are welcome under a different name; you may state that
  they are based on ALTAIS.
- Suggesting that your product, service or company is official, endorsed or
  certified by the ALTAIS project when it is not.
- Using the name or logo in a way that misleads users about where software
  comes from, especially for software that handles their encrypted data.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether a use is fine, open an issue and ask.
