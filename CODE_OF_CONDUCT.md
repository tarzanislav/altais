# Code of conduct

ALTAIS is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of
experience. We want taking part to be a good experience for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing
  in a second language.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Give and accept constructive criticism.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Respect other people's time: search before asking, and keep discussions on
  topic.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information without their consent.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or the project's work.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions and community channels, and when representing the
project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through the contact details on the
maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may temporarily or
permanently exclude people who break this code. Decisions are proportionate to
the behaviour and, where possible, start with a private warning.
